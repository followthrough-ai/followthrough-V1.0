Every promise from the call, done before the next one.

Followthrough reads your Granola meeting, finds what people committed to, and completes it: the email, the calendar invite, the Slack update, the deal stage. Exactly once, and only in the apps you allow.

Built for real inboxes

Automation you can leave switched on.

You decide when it acts for real

Start in preview and see every email, invite and update it would make, with nothing leaving your machine. Switch on Act for real when you trust it, and switch it off any time.

Real mode is on. Anything ambiguous is asked, never guessed.

Only in the apps you tick

Untick Airtable and deal updates are listed for you instead of made. Allowed apps are checked before every single action, not just at setup.

When a name is ambiguous, it asks

Every person, channel and record has to clear a 0.85 confidence gate, and near-ties are rejected. Two people called Priya means a question in your popup, never an email to the wrong one.

Exactly once, even when the network lies

Each action claims an idempotency key before it calls the app. Timeouts and rate limits are retried; a response lost after the email already went out is never re-sent, even across a restart.

Every run leaves a trail you can replay

One JSONL trace per run records each step, from extraction to the final result, next to a side-effect log of what actually changed. "Why did it email that person?" has an answer.

FAQ

Before you switch it on.

Will it send anything without me?

Only when Act for real is switched on in the extension (with a confirmation) or FOLLOWTHROUGH_MODE=real is set. Otherwise every run is a preview: you see what it would do and nothing is sent.

What do I need to run it?

Python 3.10 or newer, Chrome or Edge, a Granola Business or Enterprise plan for API access, and a Groq API key (the free tier works). Then connect the apps you want it to use.

What happens if it isn't sure who someone is?

It asks. Matches below the 0.85 confidence gate, or two candidates that score almost the same, become a question in the popup. Nothing is done for that commitment until you answer.

Can the same email go out twice?

No. Each action is keyed by what it does (app, action and target), and the key is claimed before the app is called. Re-running a meeting or restarting the engine finds the key and skips the action.

Where does my meeting data go?

The engine runs on your computer and only talks to the services you configure. Transcripts are sent to Groq for extraction, so check your organisation's data policy before using real customer calls.
